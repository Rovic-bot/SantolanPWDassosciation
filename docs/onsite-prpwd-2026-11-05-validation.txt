Implemented
- Save and open PRPWD opens a separate tab and copies the submitted PWD number.
  A blocked popup leaves the lookup marker empty; the review page offers a retry.
- The pending review has Open PRPWD and copy PWD No., Approve membership,
  Not approved and Leave pending. A changed PWD number requires another lookup.
- An authenticated admin's explicit decision on the current PWD-number form
  records the manual result, reviewer, timestamp, configured URL and note, plus
  separate verification and membership-decision audits in one transaction.
- Both verification and membership-decision permissions are enforced server-side.
  Lookup markers only associate the opened lookup with the reviewed number;
  they are not claims that the government site loaded or returned a result.
- Merely saving registration/opening the link creates no Member or verification.
  Existing references, original declarations, private attachments, idempotent
  retries, stale versions, duplicate-ID protection and source/audit boundaries
  remain covered. Earlier profile contracts and decisions remain retained.
- No earlier member was automatically marked verified. Existing membership
  records were compared unchanged when the link setting was updated.
