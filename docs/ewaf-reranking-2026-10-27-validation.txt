Implemented behavior
New composite SES calculations retain an explicit
composite-post-cutoff-fulfillment-v1 source contract. An already calculated
pool can reuse the exact accepted review when the only differences are later
confirmed deliveries, supported by native delivery audits and original
beneficiary approvals. The new snapshot retains both the reviewed source and
the actual current source, plus receipt/audit/approval witnesses. No stored
member, application, assessment, decision, receipt or earlier run is edited.

Only releases both delivered after the cutoff date and recorded after the
cutoff instant qualify for this reconciliation. Backdated/in-period changes,
voids, altered approval provenance, unexplained application version/status
changes, altered source information, unresolved fields and changed supporting
evidence remain blockers. New or unreviewed pools do not inherit acceptance.
The approved PA scope, history period, coverage finding, formulas and counting
rules are unchanged. The latest approved AHP weights continue to be captured
per new ranking; old calculations retain their original weights and scores.

Completed recipients remain in the original normalization/ranking pool. The
Admin table shows Assistance received and removes selection/review controls
for that completed benefit; the service also rejects duplicate assistance.
Results preparation retains a completed recipient's original selected
decision after reranking. Other decisions still require current-ranking
review. Publication remains a separate explicit action, with masked names,
full application references and private scoring/evidence data excluded.
