Program eligibility decision emails — November 6, 2026

Owner-authorized behavior
- Eligible, Needs Information, and Ineligible in Application review trigger a notification after the corresponding human decision successfully commits.
- Eligible remarks are optional. Needs Information requires an explanation of the information needed; Ineligible requires the reason. Both server-side form/service validation and browser validation enforce this, including whitespace-only input.
- Recipient precedence is the submitted application_confirmation.applicant_email, then the registered member email populated from onsite registration. With neither, the decision is saved and no message is sent. Invalid saved addresses do not silently redirect messages to another address.
- Each message contains the program, eligibility outcome, issued application reference, and supplied remarks. Eligible messages distinguish eligibility from beneficiary selection and actual assistance. No scores, disability details, attachments, or public tracking links are added.

Delivery and privacy
- Uses the existing Association Gmail connection from Community Assistance Desk email settings, including encrypted credentials and sender-bound connection checks. No second provider or credential store was introduced.
- Notification content, recipient/source, sender, decision version, requesting administrator, and each delivery attempt are retained privately. One job is allowed per application version. The send is registered with transaction.on_commit and claims its job before contacting the provider.
- Failed delivery preserves the decision. Authorized reviewers can retry the saved email without changing eligibility. Retries retain the original recipient/message, enforce current application version, reject already accepted/in-flight messages, and record an audit event. An unconfirmed attempt may be manually retried after five minutes with a duplicate-delivery notice. Queued messages superseded by application changes are skipped.
- A provider success is labeled Accepted by email service, not proof of inbox delivery. Synthetic memory capture is labeled as a test outbox. Provider response bodies and secrets are not retained in attempt logs.
- The review page shows the chosen address/source and latest notification state. The public application email field explains the notification/fallback behavior. Existing eligibility, evidence, selection, release, and permission rules remain.
