Proposal versus calculated AHP weights
No mathematical engine or weight policy was modified. ewaf/weight_input.py
forms pairwise ratios from the slider proposal, maps each to the supported
Saaty 1-9 scale (or reciprocal), and runs the approved AHP calculation and
consistency checks. Nearest means minimum multiplicative error. For example,
2.5:1 maps to 3:1; an exact 5:1 remains 5:1. Final percentages may therefore
differ from the proposal. Display rounding also apportions hundredths to make
the displayed total 100%; full precision remains in the approved calculation.
The approved calculated weights remain the weights used for new rankings.

Protected workflow
Generation retains cutoff, eligibility, current-evidence, current-run, slots,
configuration, authority, source freshness and exact-tie checks. An unresolved
capacity-boundary tie cannot be silently finalized. Deferred or older-run
decisions require an explicit review. Finalization uses record_decision;
publication uses the existing prepare_results and publish_results services.
The version and a digest of the reviewed run, decisions, applications and
projection reject changed-page submissions. All finalization, result-revision
and publication writes share a transaction; failures roll back together.
CSRF protection remains. Unauthorized selection/publication is rejected.
A publication-only staff member with dashboard access can publish completed
decisions without private scoring access or permission to choose beneficiaries.
They cannot finalize undecided applicants. The earlier private draft POST and
separate publisher review remain available for existing workflows.
