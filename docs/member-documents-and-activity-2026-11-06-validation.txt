Member documents, editor parity, and activity width — 6 November 2026

Scope
- Display existing Association onsite uploads on the member record.
- Include the onsite document fields and acknowledgement in Edit profile.
- Give Program applications and Assistance history the full record width.

Cause and implementation
- The member page previously read only MembershipApplicationDocument. Current
  onsite registration files belong to RegistrationAttachment revisions; affected
  records had active uploads but no rows in the legacy document table.
- The member page now resolves current onsite uploads through their existing
  protected routes, together with legacy documents and later member uploads.
- Intake and member edit forms share the 2x2 photo, Birth OR Marriage Certificate,
  Voter's Certificate, and civil-document choice fields and validators. The editor
  requires a fresh acknowledgement and displays the privacy notice and current
  files. Leaving an upload blank retains its file. Physical envelope handling is
  unchanged; no envelope upload was introduced.
- New member uploads use immutable MemberDocumentUpload versions, private
  preview/download routes, existing document permissions, and MemberEvent audits.
  Replacements retain earlier files and original registration declarations.
  Profile snapshots and existing source invalidation include the new versions.
  Late transaction/audit failure removes newly stored files.
- The activity section is now a direct full-width grid child in the shared
  member detail template, used by the full page and the member dialog.
- The shared onsite upload filename/remove controls also initialize in the
  member editor, without requiring the intake submission review dialog.
