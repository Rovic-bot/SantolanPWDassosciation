## Member screening and confirmed application

- The public flow verifies the existing control number, registered full name, birthdate and contact number without creating an account. It requires an active, unarchived member with a recorded approved intake and decision authority. It uses conservative name/phone normalization, shared rate limits and a signed, short-lived server session grant. Membership, classifications, program version and authorization are rechecked at submission.
- Classification screening uses the internal member record. At least one current allowed classification passes; additional classifications do not disqualify the member or add points. Missing, inactive or unresolved classification data receives a correction/Help Desk outcome rather than an inferred failure.
- An exact identity match with a confirmed classification mismatch receives **Confirmed requirement not met**, the program-requirement explanation and review/help links. It creates neither a submitted application nor a reference or normal approval-queue entry. An unsuccessful membership match receives the requested generic **Unable to confirm / correctable information** guidance and does not prove non-membership or reveal the failed field.
- **Screening passed** leads to the application form for Admin review. It is expressly distinct from final eligibility or beneficiary approval. Other mandatory program requirements still apply.
- The form collects name, contact number, optional email, PWD ID number, current-information confirmation and any change/uncertainty explanation. Name/contact prefill comes only from the details just entered during verification; the form does not expose a stored private profile or automatically insert the database email. The supplied email is independently validated and need not match the member record.
- The confirmation asks about employment, household monthly income, household situation and number of household members. Change/uncertainty requires an explanation. These declarations are retained in the new application's private source snapshot, linked to the matched member. They never overwrite accepted member values, evidence or scores.
- Changed/uncertain information starts in the existing Needs Information state, with the required identity-evidence review still pending. Unchanged declarations do not renew an evidence date; suitable current accepted documents can still be reused through the existing document workflow.
- Submission remains atomic and idempotent, with immutable references that contain no applicant name. Receipts preserve copy/print instructions and now explicitly explain Print → Save as PDF for downloading a copy. Existing receipt QR and verified tracking access remain available. Receipt, eligibility or score does not guarantee assistance.

## Evidence review, evaluation and decisions

- Applications & Verification shows the submitted contact/identity details and current-information report to authorized staff. A changed/uncertain report links to a protected evidence-review page.
- Resolution requires the existing application/member/eligibility-review and document-view permissions, current accepted supporting evidence belonging to that member, a fresh review token, an explanation and explicit confirmation of the program's applicable reference period and cutoff. Staff must use the existing accepted-record and document-review workflows for actual corrections first.
- The resolution records evidence provenance, member/source version, applicable policy, actor and reason in the existing audit log. It does not directly write employment, household income, household size, criterion values, scores, ranks or selection outcomes. Audit failure rolls back the operation; stale/repeated submissions and unrelated or outdated evidence are rejected. Final/closed cases retain their existing return-for-review requirements.
- Unresolved reports block eligibility confirmation, EWAF readiness and the decision/release preconditions. Resolution changes the application's source context so old criterion assessments and calculations cannot be silently reused. Later material member, evidence or policy changes require renewed review. Historical applications without this declaration keep their original source-context shape and history.
- The retained workflow remains: screening → Admin evidence review → eligibility confirmed → approved EWAF when applicable → authorized human beneficiary decision → separately authorized privacy-protected Results → actual release. Calculations and configuration stay in Admin; public pages explain the process and direct corrections to the Help Desk or Resources & Help's referral-support service.
