Implementation
- ProgramForm allows a date-only preflight; direct ModelForm saves remain guarded.
- save_program delegates supported corrections to the schedule service under the
  existing terms lock and an atomic transaction, checking the version and reason.
- Existing applications must remain within the registration interval. External
  approvals, accepted scoring inputs, calculations and final outcomes keep their
  schedules protected. Mixed edits to other protected terms remain rejected.
- Existing application snapshots/references and historical configurations stay
  unchanged. The update appends a configuration with the same approved policy.
  A cutoff equal to the former registration close follows the new close; a
  separately scheduled cutoff stays fixed. Policy validity remains enforced.
- Weights-only intake retains its exact inherited weight version and appends a
  new source binding. Non-scored programs do not acquire scoring configuration.
- The program audit and appended configuration roll back together on failure.
