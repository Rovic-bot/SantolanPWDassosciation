Persistent EWAF applicant inputs and selection panel - 2026-11-06

Owner request
Show applicant inputs on the right beside the ranking, remove row Review buttons
and the inputs section below the table. Owner clarified that Select for
assistance should move into the right-side panel, not be removed.

Implemented
- Desktop ranking and a sticky, independently scrollable applicant panel share
  one grid. The first visible ranked applicant is selected by default. Clicking
  an applicant name or row updates the panel and selected-row highlight without
  a modal. The old Review action column and drawer template are removed.
- A labeled applicant selector also exposes pending/excluded/unranked applicants
  whose inputs otherwise would not appear in the scored table. Current inputs,
  source/status badges and member/application links retain their existing values.
- The selected applicant's saved score and existing selection forms are in the
  same panel. No viewing or switching action submits a beneficiary decision.
  Permission, current-ranking, tie, registration-window, revision and capacity
  checks remain in the existing services. Excluded applicants have no selection
  form. Unranked applicants do not inherit another applicant's score/actions.
- Invalid submissions retain their applicant and bound errors. Successful
  selection returns to the same applicant. Filters reset the panel to a matching
  row; empty results show an empty panel with an all-applicant selector.
- Named links and GET forms work without JavaScript. Progressive row switching
  refreshes server-rendered HTML with cancellation of superseded requests and
  restores browser back/forward behavior. The small-screen layout stacks the
  panel; row activation focuses it and scrolls it into view. Bottom space keeps
  selection controls clear of the floating Display widget.
- Score-breakdown links and retired input bookmarks lead to the same panel.
  No schema, scoring, ranking, policy or stored decision changes.
