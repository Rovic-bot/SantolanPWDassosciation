Next steps

  1. 1

    Wait for Admin review and check your email

    The Association will email you an invitation for office verification. Your application remains pending and you are not yet a member. Check your inbox and spam folder.

  2. 2

    Visit the Association office with your PWD ID

    Bring your PWD ID Card, application reference or QR code, and the required membership documents to the Barangay Santolan PWD Association Help Desk.

  3. 3

    Complete verification and wait for membership confirmation

    Review your saved information with the administrator and present your PWD ID Card. After verification and final Admin approval, a separate email will confirm that you are now a member of the Association.
