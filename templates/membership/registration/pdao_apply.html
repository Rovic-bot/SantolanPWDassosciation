Printing, privacy and requirements

Complete the fields from the supplied Pasig City PDAO Version 4.0 form. Submit below to prepare a printable copy, then sign and bring it with your requirements for in-person verification.

Attach your 1×1 photo and sign the printed form. Leave unknown or inapplicable optional fields blank; do not guess medical details or identification numbers. Age is calculated from your date of birth.

Privacy information

Your entries are sent to this system only to display the printable form in this response. They are not saved as a registration record, stored in your session, or forwarded to PDAO. Print or save your copy before leaving. On a shared device, close this tab and protect or remove saved copies after use.

The supplied PDAO form includes its own consent statement for PDAO collection, processing, storage and retention. Read it on the printout and ask PDAO about its privacy practices before signing and submitting onsite.
