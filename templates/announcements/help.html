How to use announcements

General association news

  1. Choose the + button beside the filters to create an announcement. Enter the title and approved public text. If adding an image, provide an image description.
  2. Save the private draft and review its content preview.
  3. In Publication controls, choose Publish on the public website, confirm and apply the change.

Official program results

  1. In Programs, enter and authorize the approved selection method and calendar-photo instructions. For an already published program without authorization, choose Authorize program policy in its Publication controls.
  2. Wait until applications close. Complete each application’s required reviews. Eligible applications need a current official selection decision; other applications need a confirmed final outcome. Resolve Needs Information and pending reviews.
  3. Choose Prepare program results, select the program and continue. Enter a private preparation reason, confirm and choose Prepare result draft.
  4. Review the masked names, reference endings, selected participants, slots, eligible count and selection explanation. Check for small-group or contextual privacy risks.
  5. In the announcement’s Publication controls, choose Publish on the public website, confirm the privacy review and choose Apply publication change.

EWAF programs: their result workflow is unavailable in this version. These results must wait for current EWAF calculation, ranking and authorized selection. Keep the approved program method; do not disable EWAF or type a participant list into a general announcement to bypass the requirement. General association news remains available.

Corrections: correct and review the source records first, then choose Prepare a new result revision. This withdraws the old publication until the new preview is reviewed and authorized. History remains. Stale results are withheld automatically.

Use Withdraw to draft or Archive announcement to remove a notice from public view. Publish only approved public content. Full names, full references, scores, documents and photos remain private; ambiguous masked identities are resolved through the Help Desk.

If a control is missing, your account may lack the required permission. Ask the authorized system administrator; publication and source review have separate permissions.
