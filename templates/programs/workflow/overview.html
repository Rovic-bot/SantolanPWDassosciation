How program applications work

  1. Read the program purpose, slots, schedule, eligibility requirements, supporting documents and approved selection method.
  2. Verify Association membership. Screening uses the classifications stored in the internal member record; one allowed match is enough.
  3. Confirm your name, contact number and optional email. Report changes to employment, household monthly income or household membership, and provide the required evidence.
  4. Keep the application reference and receipt. Admin reviews identity, documents and other mandatory requirements before confirming eligibility.
  5. Eligible applications undergo EWAF using approved, locked rules. Authorized personnel make the final beneficiary decisions; approved outcomes may be published in Program Results announcements.

The program uses accepted information applicable to its evaluation—not automatically the information recorded on the membership registration date. New declarations need evidence review before they can change evaluation inputs. Suitable current accepted records can be reused.

For corrections or review, contact the Association Help Desk or use Community Assistance Desk. Reports do not update accepted records automatically.

A successful application, eligibility confirmation or EWAF score does not guarantee selection. Selection and actual receipt of assistance are recorded separately.
