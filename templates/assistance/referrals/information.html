How it works

  1. Verify your membership using your registered details. No member account is needed.
  2. Provide your email address, subject, assistance type and concern. Select Add supporting evidence to attach one file if available; you may provide it later by email or onsite.
  3. Keep the unique reference number shown after submission. An acknowledgment containing your request details and reference is sent from the Association email address.
  4. Authorized personnel review your ticket and provide updates or further instructions by email or at the Help Desk.

Choose Continue a conversation and enter your private conversation reference to return. Keep it private. No member account is needed.

Your information

Your request, contact details and supporting evidence are kept private for authorized Association personnel to process and follow up. Request details and updates are sent to the email address you provide. Supporting files are kept in the private system and are not attached to acknowledgment emails. Share only evidence relevant to your concern. Contact the Association Help Desk about corrections or privacy requests.
